The story Explore Order

Simulated data for one small grocery: 70 products, 10 vendors, records from 2 Jun 2026 and a 90-day review period from 1 Aug to 29 Oct 2026, with “today” as 29 Oct. Only records a store keeps are used: daily sales and stock, purchase orders (some missing or late) and goods receipts. Lost sales are estimates and lean low. Every figure traces to a sheet and cell in the workbook Bakalah_Demo_Data_v3.xlsx. No real store, company or figures.
